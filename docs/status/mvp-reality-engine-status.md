## MVP gate

The product is MVP-complete only when:

- a fresh browser uploads an authorized real football MP4;
- real perception reconstructs the event into SWM;
- actual video artifacts are produced for Original, Tactical, 3D Game and Anime/NPR;
- the artifacts persist and play as normal video;
- the Reality Switcher swaps between outputs for the same session;
- at least two materially different real clips pass;
- a human confirms the outputs are recognizably the same underlying event and meaningfully different visual realities.
